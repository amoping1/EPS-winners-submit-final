How a forecast is produced

Two of the six steps are agents that decide their own next move. The other four are fixed code, deliberately: the last word on a number belongs to something that cannot be argued round.

Agents (decide for themselves)

  • Research agent — writes its own queries, reads, re-queries when evidence is thin
  • Three forecasters — guidance, statistical, qualitative; separate contexts
  • Critic — sees the numbers but not the reasoning, tries to refute

Fixed code (cannot be argued round)

  • Retrieval — BM25 with a hard as_of date cutoff
  • Aggregator — sorts evidence into channels, flags thin metrics
  • Reconcile — confidence weighting, outliers cut
  • Rails — units, basis, period, guidance bound, history clamp

Why consensus matters

  • Score is our error ÷ Wall Street’s error
  • Consensus is therefore the benchmark, not a hint
  • Matching it scores ~1.0; beating it needs a reason
  • Hays is the one place we deviate deliberately

Where this is weakest

Known failures

  • Segment operating profit is the weakest metric — 77% error in backtest. It lives in segment notes with the thinnest history and no consensus coverage.
  • The critic has no teeth. It objects and nothing acts on the objection; the refute-and-re-run loop was designed and never built.
  • Hays basic EPS rests on two historical points, so no trend can be fitted and the clamp cannot engage.

Tried and abandoned

  • A deterministic guidance scanner to match the consensus scanner. Filings spell quarters out, guidance spans sentences, magnitudes mix billions and millions. It returned nothing twice and was cut rather than shipped half-working.
  • A flat median across the three forecasters — discarded once it became clear guidance quality varies enormously between these four companies.

Honest limits

  • Consensus-anchoring targets a score near 1.0. Beating Wall Street needs a reason to deviate; we have one only for Hays, where management guided to the top of a published range.
  • Industry profiles are agnostic by construction and demonstrated on four sectors. Never run on a fifth; the fallback profile has no coverage.
  • Three of four fiscal period ends are derived arithmetically, not read from a document stating them.
  • Runs vary. Rails hold a number steady where they engage; where no anchor surfaces, it wanders.
